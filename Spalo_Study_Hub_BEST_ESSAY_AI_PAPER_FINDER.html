About Spalo

My name is Sbongumusa Maphanga, known as Spalo. I created this learning project because I believe young people deserve access to useful knowledge, practical skills and encouragement to build a better future.

This website brings school learning and future-focused skills together in one place. It is designed to help learners understand their subjects, practise questions, learn from mistakes and develop the discipline needed beyond school.

🎯 Purpose of this website

The purpose is to help young people learn, grow and work towards financial freedom. Education is the foundation. After building strong school knowledge, young people can explore careers, entrepreneurship, technology, coding, money management and other legitimate ways of creating opportunities.

Financial freedom is not a promise or a shortcut. It comes from education, useful skills, discipline, good decisions, saving, responsible investing and creating value over time.

Use this website to study first, build your skills, and prepare yourself for the future.

✅ Research checklist

  1. Write your research question.
  2. Use more than one reliable source where possible.
  3. Check the author, organisation, date and evidence.
  4. Compare conflicting information.
  5. Write notes in your own words.
  6. Keep a record of the sources you used.

✍️ Spalo Essay Centre

Plan, practise and improve school essays.

📝 Essay structure

Introduction: Introduce the topic and your main position or focus.

Body: Develop one main idea per paragraph with evidence/examples and explanation.

Conclusion: Summarise the main points and give a clear closing statement.

💡 Exam Tips

  • Read every question carefully before answering.
  • Show calculations in Mathematics and Physical Sciences.
  • Use correct units and significant figures where required.
  • For languages, plan your answer before writing.
  • For essays, use an introduction, logical paragraphs and a conclusion.
  • Do past papers under timed conditions.
  • Keep a mistake notebook and revise it weekly.
